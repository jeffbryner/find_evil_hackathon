*Scenario* 

The Stark Enterprise Intelligence Division detected several internal documents posted on a Chinese university research file share. The documents appear to have been posted to the Chinese server on June 22-23, 2016\.  They were clearly intellectual property and possibly trade secrets that should never have been released outside Stark Enterprise control. They included research on calculations of cell regrowth, rapid cell regeneration research, and ZF DNA splice test notes.  The Stark Intelligence division identified an employee, Anthony Vanko, who is known to have been doing research on these topics, including modifying Zebrafish DNA. 

Coincidentally, Vanko is also the subject of a recent security event triggered by abnormal network file server activity traced to his account.  On June 30, 2016, the automated network monitoring artificial intelligence system (JARVIS) detected a large volume of data transferred from the StarkResearch server to Vanko’s workstation.  JARVIS detected data being copied from the following directories on the StarkResearch server: 

\\StarkResearch\\Level 5 Classified\\  
\\StarkResearch\\Level 6 Classified\\  
\\StarkResearch\\Level 7 Classified\\  
\\StarkResearch\\Level 8 Classified\\

Following the suspicious activity detected on June 30, 2016, JARVIS suspended Vanko’s network account and sent an email to Vanko informing him the level of his recent computer and network activity had been deemed suspicious, indicating an 82.3% likelihood of attempted intellectual property theft from Stark Enterprises. Jarvis also informed Vanko his account was suspended. 

Stark Enterprises has requested a digital investigative analysis be conducted to determine if Vanko was involved with the intentional dissemination of classified information and research, including but not limited to the documents discovered on the Chinese University server.  Investigators are also asked to validate if Vanko was involved in copying a large volume of classified research information from the StarkResearch server and if so, what was done with the data. 

In Summary, we need you to try and determine:

* Was Vanko involved with the dissemination of classified information?  
* Validate if Vanko copying large volume from StarkResearch server  
* What was done with the data?


*Additional Background*

Anthony Vanko is the lead biochemical engineer for Stark Enterprises, a company that specializes in defense and medical research development. Vanko’s lab is located in the Stark Enterprise DC Research and Development Facility, dedicated to research of rapid cell regeneration development through chemical enhancement. The goal of Vanko’s lab is to develop a new drug that significantly increases the regeneration any damaged cell in the human body. The military plans to weaponize the drug to create a super soldier incapable of being incapacitated or killed by conventional or biological weapons. 

Vanko travels around the world conducting research on plants and animals that display a higher than normal cell regeneration.  Vanko was issued a Surface 3 computer running Windows 10 Professional.  He utilizes wireless networks as he travels and routinely connects to the Stark Enterprise wireless network (StarkLabs).  With a portable network device, he can connect to the Stark satellites via 802.11 (StarkSat).  Vanko carries an iPhone 5c.  Stark corporate records indicate he does not use the phone much for either calling or the cellular data plan, but the device may have been synced to his Surface laptop. Like all smartphones, the device is capable of connecting to wireless networks so Vanko’s true data usage is unknown.  Vanko is known to communicate using a variety of methods including email, Skype, and WhatsApp. 

Vanko recently received his annual review and was advised that he has not been one of the top lab performers and is not getting an annual bonus.  Vanko was also told that his lab funding is being reduced because Stark Enterprises cannot sell the results of his research on the open market because the U.S. military wants exclusive use. 

Due to the degradation of the U.S. political climate, Congress has become impotent to pass any legislation to benefit the pharmaceutical research community or increase military spending.  On June 25, 2016, CEO Tony Stark sent an email to several research labs within Stark Enterprises informing them that due to the unpredictable U.S. political environment, all non-weapon system research department budgets were being reduced by 25%. 

Internal corporate interviews with Vanko’s only known friend, Kylie Normandy, a scientist at Stark Enterprise’s West Coast research facility, revealed she speaks with Vanko occasionally and he recently seemed unhappy that Stark Enterprises was not appropriately funding his research. Kylie stated she had met Vanko at a local bar in downtown Washington DC one Friday evening while visiting on approximately the third week of June 2016\. Kylie stated she did not talk much with Vanko at the bar as she brought a female acquaintance who was working on her doctorate in chemical engineering and Vanko and her friend hit it off and left the bar together.
